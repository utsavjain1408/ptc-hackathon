Stop malicious packages before they reach your build with GitHub Package Firewall
Product Demo
In-person
AI summary
A wave of recent high-profile attacks has exploited a single weakness: the privileges baked into CI/CD pipelines. An attacker who lands a malicious package in your build can steal the credentials your runner holds, or inject malware that ships to everyone downstream. Securing the build environment has never mattered more. GitHub Package Firewall sits between your build and upstream package registries, screening dependencies before they ever reach your runner, so a compromised package is blocked at the door instead of executing with your pipeline's privileges. It's a new service and free to use. In this session, you'll see how the firewall works, how to put it in front of your own builds, and how it protects the users who depend on what you ship.
